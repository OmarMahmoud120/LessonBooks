# Vision

LessonBooks helps a parent turn a book they have the right to use into private, interactive lessons. The parent remains the reviewer. Children receive no accounts and no public publishing surface.

Initial scope: England, English National Curriculum, English, Years 1–2, mathematics. This is an explicit default, not an inferred user location. Three to five lessons per accepted PDF, with five activity types. Arabic is an RTL test fixture, not a second launch curriculum.

Success: a parent signs in, records consent, creates a pseudonymous profile, uploads a PDF, sees asynchronous progress, reviews and approves valid lessons, and previews the same player on web and mobile. No real-data launch until legal, region, retention and spending decisions are approved.

Phases 2–5 remain out of scope: assignments, kid gate, durable offline downloads, progress dashboards, additional curriculum launches, editing/regeneration UX, game shells and billing.
