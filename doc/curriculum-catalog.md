# Curriculum catalog

Initial seed: country GB-ENG, curriculum england-national-2014, language en-GB, subject mathematics, grades year-1 and year-2. Grade and curriculum IDs are stable; display labels are localizable. Age bands are guidance, not proof of a child's age or reading ability.

Seed data lives in doc/curricula. Validate unique IDs, valid grade/subject relationships and references from profiles/lessons. Catalog expansion follows .agents/skills/add-curriculum/SKILL.md.

Authoritative reference: [Department for Education mathematics programmes](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study), checked 2026-11-03. This product is not endorsed or accredited by that authority. The initial learning objectives are narrow arithmetic/counting topics, not a claim of complete curriculum coverage.

Arabic sample lessons validate language direction and text rendering only. A supported language and a supported curriculum are independent dimensions.
